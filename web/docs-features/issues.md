# Issues and sketches

BCF is the container and the markup drawing engine is the drawing layer. There is one drawing system (clouds, arrows, text, callouts, shapes and pen, attached to the model in 3D or view-locked), and two things are built on it:

- **Sketches** (formerly markup sets): a saved view, its drawings and an optional one-line note. They keep the 2D markup view and the PDF report. They have **no status and no comment thread**.
- **Issues** (BCF topics): status, priority, assignee, due date, comments, history. **An issue's viewpoints are sketches.** Raising a sketch as an issue makes an issue (or adds the sketch to an existing one) whose viewpoint links to the sketch by id, not a copy.

Where: the sidebar tab **Issues** (after Model, Sets and Views) with the switch `Issues · Sketches · Sheets` (the third segment lists PDF drawing sheets and the sketches drawn on them, [sheets.md](sheets.md)); the drawing toolbar floats over the view while a sketch is edited; the status bar says `N open issues`; the top-bar search finds issues and sketches. Scripting: `window.__xrayIssues` (panel, links, search, migration), `window.__xrayBcf` (issues as BCF data), `window.__xrayMarkup` (sketches). Code: `src/issues/` (`link.ts`, `search.ts` and `derivedViews.ts` pure, `controller.ts`, `derivedSwitch.ts`, `viewSwitchUi.ts`, `ui.ts`), `src/plugins/issues.ts`, `src/markup/toolbar.ts`. See [markup.md](markup.md) for the drawing engine and the PDF, [bcf.md](bcf.md) for the file formats.

## The panel

`Issues` segment: `New issue` (the old topic dialog), `Open BCF`, a search box, the chips `All / Open / Closed` (with counts), and the list of the loaded model's issues with status, priority, number of sketches, comments and assignee. `Other models (n)` lists the issues made on another model, hidden and not drawn, with `Open`, `Move to this model` (the issue and its sketches) and `Delete issue`, as before ([markup.md](markup.md), "Which model an item belongs to"). Clicking an issue opens its detail in the same column and applies the view of its first viewpoint the BCF way (camera, selection, hides, colours, clip planes; `Clear` undoes it); the sketch of the applied viewpoint is drawn next to the active sketch (`MarkupController.companion`), so applying a viewpoint from a script or a link shows its drawing too; `‹ Issues` goes back.

The issue detail: snapshot with the drawing burned in, status and priority lists, assignee, due date, the sketches (one row per viewpoint: `Apply`, `Clear`, `Edit drawing`, `Open sketch`; `Edit drawing` on a viewpoint without a drawing makes its sketch, linked to the issue), `Add current view as sketch`, `Place pin` / `Remove pin`, the comments with `Add comment`, the history (created, each comment, last change), and `Export BCF 2.1` / `Export BCF 3.0` / `Delete issue`.

`Sketches` segment: `New sketch` (saves the current view and starts drawing), `Open file` (a sketch PDF or JSON), a search box (title and note), cards with thumbnails, and `Other models (n)`. The card of a sketch that belongs to an issue says `Issue: <title>`. The sketch detail: thumbnail, title, note, `Raise as issue` (a list picks `New issue` or `Add to: <issue>`), `Edit drawing`, `Restore view`, `Update view`, `Export PDF`, `Export JSON`, page size and picture quality for the PDF, `Delete sketch`, and the list of drawings (number, kind, text, the attachment chip of the selected one). A sketch that belongs to an issue shows `Viewpoint of the issue <title>` with `Open issue`: the link shows from both sides.

Phone (up to 700 px wide): the sidebar collapses and the panel is a **bottom sheet** (like the classification review walk). It steps aside while a sketch is edited and comes back on `Done`; the cross closes it, `N open issues` in the status bar opens it.

## Sheets

`Sheets` segment: `Open PDF`, then each PDF with its pages as thumbnails, `Export marked-up PDF`, `Remove`, and under each page its sketches (`Edit drawing`, `Raise as issue`, `JSON`, `Delete`; a read-only `Imported annotations` sketch has `Copy to editable sketch`). A page opens the sheet view over the 3D area (`Back to model` returns). A sketch on a sheet is a markup set with a sheet frame; it is not listed in the Sketches grid. Raising it as an issue works as for any sketch: the viewpoint is a snapshot of the sheet region, the topic gets a document reference to the PDF and the side file names the sheet. See [sheets.md](sheets.md).

## Plan and Section views

A sketch whose drawings are **tied to a 3D construct** has a switch `3D · Plan · Section`. A drawing is tied when its attachment is `surface`, `elements`, `measurement`, `penetration`, `region` or `point` ([markup.md](markup.md), "Attachments"). View-locked drawings (`none`) and foreign `lines` do not tie a sketch, so a sketch with only those has no switch. The same control sits in the issue detail (under the row of each tied sketch), in the sketch detail and in the drawing toolbar (when the active sketch is tied). On a phone it fits 360 px.

- **3D**: the sketch's own camera, no clip planes of the switch. It also puts back the section you had.
- **Plan**: an orthographic camera looking straight down, north up. One horizontal cut at the storey level + 1.2 m. A target that lies wholly above that cut (ceiling MEP) is cut 0.3 m above its top. A short target (up to 1.5 m) that the cut would pass through is cut 0.05 m above its top, so it shows whole. Taller targets (walls, columns) are cut at 1.2 m like a plan drawing. The storey is the one of the target element; without storeys it is the highest level at or below the target centre, else the target base. The view frames the target at 4 times its larger side, at least 6 m across.
- **Section**: an orthographic vertical cut through the target centroid. The cut is perpendicular to the horizontal part of the surface normal when that is clearly horizontal (within 30 degrees of the horizontal plane; the view looks into the surface), else perpendicular to the longer horizontal side of the target (the view looks along it, from the side of the authored camera). A back plane `max(2 m, 2 x the target depth)` behind the cut keeps the depth short. The view frames the target at 3 times its larger across or height, at least 5 m.

What the target is: the stroke of a surface or element drawing on the model as it lies now, the end points of a measurement, the overlap of a penetration's pipe and host (else both), the region box, the anchor of a text box. With a drawing selected, the live views use that drawing; otherwise the union of the tied drawings. Everything is derived from the data: nothing is stored on the sketch.

Plan and Section use the pseudo-orthographic projection of the viewer (2 degree field of view, the camera moved back, as the `P` key does) and `renderer.setClipPlanes` with caps, the way a BCF viewpoint applies its planes. The attached drawings follow, because an attachment projects in any camera. A section you had (the section tool's box or plane, or the planes of an applied BCF viewpoint) is remembered when you leave 3D for the first time and put back when you press 3D. Planes that someone sets after the switch (you move the section tool) stay. Leaving the orthographic projection by hand (`P`, a saved view) ends the switch as the control shows it. The view switch is one camera move in the global undo stack (the camera watch records it); the clip planes are not on that stack. Pure derivation: `src/issues/derivedViews.ts`; viewer side: `src/issues/derivedSwitch.ts`; control: `src/issues/viewSwitchUi.ts`. Scripting: `__xrayIssues.viewMode(id)`, `setViewMode(id, mode)`, `viewSwitchAvailable(id)`, `derivedView(id, 'plan' | 'section')`.

**BCF export.** Every export (and so every issue raised from a tied sketch and exported) also writes the Plan and the Section of each tied sketch as two more viewpoints of the topic, right after the sketch's viewpoint: an `OrthogonalCamera` with `ViewToWorldScale` (metres of visible height) and `AspectRatio` (3.0), `ClippingPlanes` (`Direction` points into the cut-away half), and the Selection, Visibility, Coloring and 3D `Lines` of the source viewpoint. They have no snapshot. Other BCF tools read them as ordinary orthographic viewpoints with clipping planes. They are made at export from the current sketch, not stored in the session, so an edited sketch always exports fresh views. The side file marks each one: `"derived": { "kind": "plan" | "section", "sketch": "<sketch id>", "of": "<viewpoint guid>" }`. Opening the file makes the sketch of the source viewpoint only: a derived viewpoint is a view of that sketch and never gets a sketch (`sketchFromViewpoint` refuses it). The issue detail lists it as `Plan (derived)` or `Section (derived)` with `Apply` and `Clear`. Exporting again replaces the derived viewpoints (their guids stay) and never doubles them; derived viewpoints of a file whose source has no tied sketch here are written back as they are.

## The drawing toolbar

A small floating bar, shown only while a sketch or an issue's viewpoint is edited (`window.__xrayMarkup.editing`; `Edit drawing`, `New sketch` or choosing a tool in a script start it, `Done` ends it). It has all the tools of the markup tool (select, cloud, cloud draw, callout, text, pointer, marker, highlight box, rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow, polyline, polygon, pen), the colours, the line weights, `Fill`, the text of the next callout, `Undo` and `Redo` of the sketch's markup history, `Finish` for a polyline in progress and `Done`. When the active sketch is tied to a 3D construct the bar has one more row: `3D · Plan · Section` (see "Plan and Section views"). It sits bottom centre inside the edge chrome and moves aside or up where another reserved rect is (the gizmo, legends), through the shared overlay reserve API (`ov.reserve('markup-float', ...)`, `toolbarSpot` in `src/markup/toolbar.ts`); it never covers the gizmo or the top bar, and wraps to fit 390 px.

The global undo stack skips while the toolbar is active (`markupHasKeys` in `src/plugins/undo.ts` checks `editing`). Pointer modes Arrow and Hand keep working: a drawing tool takes the left button, other buttons still orbit and pan. Keys while editing: `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Shift+Z`, `Delete`, `Escape` (cancel the drawing, then back to select, then deselect).

## Raise as issue, and how the link works

`Raise as issue` (button, `window.__xrayIssues.raise(sketchId, { topic? })`, or `window.__xrayMarkup.toBcf(sketchId)`): the sketch's view is restored, the issue is made from the title and note (status `Open`), the viewpoint captures the camera, selection, hides, colours, clip planes and a snapshot, and the viewpoint gets `sketchId`; the sketch gets `issue: { topic, viewpoint }`. With `topic` the sketch is added to that issue as one more viewpoint.

- **One sketch, at most one issue.** Raising a sketch that already belongs to an issue is refused (`linkSketch` in `src/issues/link.ts`); open that issue instead.
- **Not a copy.** The viewpoint's drawing (`annotations`) is a projection of the sketch, rebuilt by `syncViewpoint` after every sketch change and again before every export. Shape points are fractions of the sketch camera and the viewpoint's snapshot camera is set to that same camera, so they copy over without projecting; clouds are closed, ellipses become polygons and arrows get a head. Elements that the drawing points at join the viewpoint selection (other BCF tools highlight them), as before.
- **Editing updates the issue.** Draw, move, delete, retype, undo or change an attachment: the issue's drawing follows, and `composeSnapshot` and the BCF export burn and serialise the current drawing. `Update view` on a sketch captures the issue viewpoint again from the same live view. While a viewpoint belongs to a sketch the BCF overlay draws no annotations of it (the markup view draws the sketch), so nothing is drawn twice.
- **Removing a sketch that belongs to an issue asks first** (a confirmation box in the panel, `removeSketch(id)` returns `confirm` until called with `confirmed`). The issue keeps the camera view and loses that drawing. Deleting an issue leaves its sketches as loose sketches.
- Links are repaired when topics change (`reconcileLinks`): the viewpoint's `sketchId` is the truth, `set.issue` the mirror.

## Export report (PDF and CSV)

The issues list has a collapsed block `Export report` (`src/issues/reportUi.ts`) with a **page size** (A4 or Letter, portrait) and **Plan/Section images** (both on by default) and two buttons. The report is of the list as it is filtered now: the status chip (All, Open, Closed), the search and the loaded model (issues of other models are left out). The cover says which filter it was made with. Scripts: `window.__xrayIssueReport = { pdf({ page?, planSection?, download? }), csv({ download? }), model(), options, setOptions }`.

**PDF** (`PDF report (n)`; built with the hand-written writer `src/markup/pdf.ts`, which gained links to a page and bookmarks; no dependency):

- **Cover** (page 1, and the pages after it when the list is long): model name, project name, date and author, the filter, counts (total, open, closed and a bar per status) and a table of the issues (number, title, status, priority, assignee, due date, page). Each row is a link to the issue's page.
- **One page per issue**, with a link back to the cover and bookmarks for the cover and each issue: title, status, priority, type, assignee, due date, labels, created and last changed (with the authors), the description, the **views** (the snapshot with its drawings burned in, as the detail shows it; for a sheet issue the sheet name, its PDF and page and the **region image**), the **target elements** (name, IFC class, GlobalId; the first 24, then a count), and the **comments with their author names and dates**. An issue with a long thread continues on further pages ("continued"), so "one page" holds for the usual issue only. The footer says `Page n of N`.
- **Plan and Section**: for an issue whose sketch is tied to the model (the same condition as the 3D, Plan, Section switch; sheet issues have none) a small Plan and Section sit beside the snapshot. They are rendered on the live canvas through `DerivedViewSwitch` (`src/issues/derivedSwitch.ts`, `reportRender.ts`): the issue viewpoint is applied, the switch is put on Plan and on Section and the canvas is read in the same task. A still copy of the canvas is laid over it for the whole run, so the view does not flicker, and the camera, section planes, hides and look you had are put back at the end (`window.__xrayViews` capture and restore). With the option off, no view is switched at all.
- Pictures are re-encoded as RGB JPEG (snapshots up to 1100 px, Plan and Section up to 640 px) and embedded once each.

**CSV** (`CSV (n)`): **one file, one row per issue, with a `Comments` column** (every comment as `author (date): text`, one per line inside the cell) and a `Comment count`. A second file of comments would have to be joined by hand to be of use, and a spreadsheet opens one file as it is. Columns: Number, GUID, Title, Status, Priority, Type, Assigned to, Due date, Labels, Created, Created by, Modified, Modified by, Description, Comment count, Comments, Elements (names), GlobalIds, Sheet, Sheet page, Raised from. RFC 4180 quoting, CRLF, UTF-8 with a byte order mark so a spreadsheet reads the umlauts; text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets a leading apostrophe (the formula guard of `src/tools/csv.ts`).

The comments carry author names because the person exports the file on purpose. File names: `xray-issues-<model>-<yyyymmdd-hhmm>.pdf` and `.csv`.

## Issues from checks (penetrations and clearance)

Both drawers have an `Issues` block with **Raise issues from results** (`src/issues/clashUi.ts`; the logic is in `src/issues/clash.ts` (pure) and `src/issues/clashRaise.ts`). Each result becomes an issue:

- **Title**: `Penetration: Duct-12 through Wall-3`, or `Clearance 120 mm < 300 mm: Pipe-4 / Beam-9` (the distance and the `tight (m)` limit of the run). Type `Clash`, labels `clash` and `penetration` or `clearance`, priority from the severity (penetration: no opening or an opening too small is High, unknown is Normal, a fitting opening is Low; clearance: touching is High).
- **Components**: both GlobalIds are the selection of the viewpoint. The camera frames the pair (for a long pipe beside a long beam: where they are close, `pairRegion`), with a pin and a snapshot.
- **A sketch tied to the pair**: a rectangle around the region on the viewpoint's sketch, with the attachment kind `penetration` (the MEP element and the host) for penetrations and `measurement` (a link with both GlobalIds and the distance) for clearance. It is tied, so the issue gets the 3D, Plan, Section switch and a Plan and Section in the report.
- **Which rows**: penetrations: the selected rows, else the filtered rows of the table; clearance: every clashing or tight pair of the last run (the clearance colouring must be on, with a focus).

**No doubles.** An issue is found again by **the check type plus the sorted GlobalId pair** (`clashKey`), stored on the topic as `clash` in the BCF side file (`xray-markup.json`, optional field `clash: { check, key, a, b, missing? }`), so it survives a BCF export and open. Raising after a re-run raises only the results without an issue. The block shows the counts: **new / already raised / not found in the latest run**.

**Results that no longer occur** are never deleted. An issue of the same check whose pair the latest run does not report (and that the run could have found: the pair must have been in the MEP set and the hosts it analysed, or in the focus and context it looked at; another focus or scope says nothing about it) gets one comment, `Not found in latest run`, and `clash.missing`. `Close n not found` sets the status of those that are still open to Closed. A pair that comes back gets `Found again in the latest run`.

**Batch limit.** Above **200** new results the block asks first: `Raise top 200 by severity` (penetrations: no opening, then an opening too small, then unknown, then a fitting opening, larger sizes first; clearance: the smallest distance first), `Raise all n`, or Cancel. The work runs one issue at a time with a frame between them, shows `Raising issues d / n` and has a `Stop` button, and the issues panel does not jump into the detail of each new issue (`IssuesController.batch`). Scripts: `window.__xrayClash = { config: { maxBatch }, preview(check), raise(check, { limit? }), close(check) }`; `preview` returns `{ fresh, already, resolved }`.

## BCF import

Opening a BCF file keeps working as before (2.0, 2.1, 3.0; the side file, Lines and Components). Every viewpoint with annotations and a camera that can carry shapes becomes a **sketch linked to its issue** (`sketchFromViewpoint`): the markups are made from the annotations (callout, note, arrow, measurement callout, rectangle, freehand line as pen), numbered in order, the sketch camera is the snapshot camera, the view holds only that camera, and restoring the sketch applies the BCF viewpoint (`fromViewpoint`). A viewpoint whose drawing cannot become markup (a foreign line set without a shape) keeps its drawing as it is, drawn by the BCF overlay, and gets no sketch. The same happens to annotations added through `window.__xrayBcf.addAnnotation` and `addShape` on a viewpoint that has none yet; on a viewpoint that already is a sketch these calls (and `updateAnnotation`, `removeAnnotation`) go through the sketch (`BcfController.linked`), so a script never loses an annotation to the next projection. Until the sketch changes, the drawing of an imported viewpoint stays exactly as the file had it (no relabelling). The session link (`sketchId`, `issue`) is not written to the BCF file; the file stores the derived annotations in the side file, so re-opening an export makes the sketches again.

## Migration of old markup sets

Old sets kept review data on their markups: a comment thread, a resolved status, an assignee, a due date. Sketches have none of these any more, and nothing is lost:

- Sets with comments or a non-default status show a banner in the Sketches segment: **"N sketches have comments or a status. Raise them as issues?"** (singular: "1 sketch has comments or a status. Raise it as an issue?"). `Yes, raise them` converts each set into an issue that links to the sketch (`migrateLegacy`): title of the sketch, the note (or a generated line) as description, status `Open` when any markup was open and `Resolved` otherwise, the first assignee, the earliest due date, and every comment as an issue comment (`"<markup number>. <text>"`, original author and date, oldest first). The comments and status are then removed from the sketch. `Not now` hides the banner for the session.
- Until converted, the legacy comments and status stay in the data and show read-only on the sketch ("Earlier comments and status") and in search.
- Opening an old markup JSON (or a PDF that carries one) works as before and shows the same banner.
- `window.__xrayMarkup.comment` and the status, assignee and due fields of `update` and `add` no longer change a sketch (`comment` returns false); the data of old documents is still read and written.

## Search and status bar

The top-bar search has a section **Issues and sketches** ([search.md](search.md)): title, note or description, and comment text (also the legacy comments of sketches), every word must match, a title hit ranks above a note or description hit above a comment hit; the row says which field matched. Enter opens the tab on the item and brings its view back. `searchIssues` in `src/issues/search.ts` is pure.

The status bar shows `N open issues` (issues of the loaded model whose status is not resolved, closed, done, solved, completed, rejected, cancelled, fixed or verified; `isOpenStatus`). Clicking it opens the tab on the Issues segment with the `Open` chip.

## Keys, commands and ids

`K` (action `markup.toggle`) opens the tab on the Sketches segment. `tool.markup` and `tool.bcf` are kept as aliases (Sketches and Issues segment); `markup.new`, `markup.open`, `markup.pdf`, `markup.json`, `bcf.open`, `bcf.create`, `bcf.export`, `bcf.clear` work as before (the ones that used to need a drawer open the tab first). New: `issues.open`, `issues.sketches`, `issues.edit` (toolbar for the active sketch), `issues.done`. A packaged page whose boot config says `openTool: "bcf"` or `"markup"` (or lists them in `tools`) opens the tab.

## File formats

Additive only. The markup document stays `xray-markup-set/1`: a set may carry `note`, `issue` (`{ topic, viewpoint }`) and `fromViewpoint`; older readers ignore them and old documents read as before (comments, status, assignee, due keep reading and writing). The BCF side file gained the optional per-viewpoint `derived` marker (above); older readers ignore it. The PDF report and its attached JSON are unchanged (the table still lists legacy status and comments of a set that has them). Names, e-mail addresses and pictures of people stay out of params, share links, saved views, presets and packed pages (the leak test); the author text of sketches and issues lives in the markup JSON and the BCF file only, as before. The side file also gained an optional topic field `clash` (`{ check, key, a, b, missing? }`: the check result an issue was raised from, see "Issues from checks"); a topic with only a `clash` still gets a side file.
