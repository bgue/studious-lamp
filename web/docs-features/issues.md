# Issues

An **issue** (a BCF topic) is something your team must fix: status, priority, assignee, due date, comments and a history. It points at one or more **views**, its viewpoints. An issue is made **from a view**: `Make issue` on a view in the Views tab, or `New issue` in the Issues tab, which captures the current view first. BCF is only the exchange format.

A view is a captured moment (camera, section, selection, hides, look) that may carry drawings; a page of a PDF sheet is a view too. The Views tab lists them all: [saved-views.md](saved-views.md). The drawing engine (clouds, arrows, text, callouts, shapes and pen, attached to the model in 3D or view-locked) is in [markup.md](markup.md). The model and the mapping from the older sketches are in `docs/design/views-and-issues.md`.

Where: the sidebar tab **Issues** (after Model, Sets and Views) lists issues only. The drawing toolbar floats over the view while drawings are edited; the status bar says `N open issues`; the top-bar search finds issues, and views in its own section. Scripting: `window.__xrayIssues` (panel, links, search, migration), `window.__xrayBcf` (issues as BCF data), `window.__xrayViews` (views, `drawOn`, `makeIssue`), `window.__xrayMarkup` (the drawing layer). Code: `src/issues/` (`link.ts`, `search.ts` and `derivedViews.ts` pure, `controller.ts`, `derivedSwitch.ts`, `viewSwitchUi.ts`, `ui.ts`), `src/plugins/issues.ts`, `src/markup/toolbar.ts`, and for the list of views `src/views/unified.ts`. See [bcf.md](bcf.md) for the file formats.

## The panel

`New issue` (captures the current view, saves it in the Views list, then opens the topic dialog), `Open BCF`, a search box, the chips `All / Open / Closed` (with counts), and the list of the loaded model's issues with status, priority, number of views, comments and assignee. `Other models (n)` lists the issues made on another model, hidden and not drawn, with `Open`, `Move to this model` (the issue and its views) and `Delete issue` ([markup.md](markup.md), "Which model an item belongs to"). Clicking an issue opens its detail in the same column and applies the first view of the issue the BCF way (camera, selection, hides, colours, clip planes; `Clear` undoes it); the drawings of the applied view are drawn next to the active view (`MarkupController.companion`), so applying a viewpoint from a script or a link shows its drawings too; `‹ Issues` goes back.

The issue detail: snapshot with the drawings burned in, status and priority lists, assignee, due date, **the views of the issue** (one row per viewpoint with a thumbnail: `Apply`, `Clear`, `Edit drawings`, `Open in Views`, and the `3D · Plan · Section` switch where it exists; `Edit drawings` on a viewpoint without a drawing layer makes one, linked to the issue), `Add current view` (saves the current view into the issue), `Place pin` / `Remove pin`, the comments with `Add comment`, the history (created, each comment, last change), and `Export BCF 2.1` / `Export BCF 3.0` / `Delete issue`.

The other side of the link is in the Views tab: a view that is in an issue shows the tag `Issue` in its row and, in its detail, `In issue: <title>` with `Open issue`. See [saved-views.md](saved-views.md).

Phone (up to 700 px wide): the sidebar collapses and the Issues panel is a **bottom sheet** (like the classification review walk). It steps aside while drawings are edited and comes back on `Done`; the cross closes it, `N open issues` in the status bar opens it.

## Plan and Section views

A view whose drawings are **tied to a 3D construct** has a switch `3D · Plan · Section`. A drawing is tied when its attachment is `surface`, `elements`, `measurement`, `penetration`, `region` or `point` ([markup.md](markup.md), "Attachments"). View-locked drawings (`none`) and foreign `lines` do not tie a view, so a view with only those has no switch. The same control sits in the issue detail (under the row of each tied view), in the view detail and in the drawing toolbar (when the active view is tied). On a phone it fits 360 px.

- **3D**: the view's own camera, no clip planes of the switch. It also puts back the section you had.
- **Plan**: an orthographic camera looking straight down, north up. One horizontal cut at the storey level + 1.2 m. A target that lies wholly above that cut (ceiling MEP) is cut 0.3 m above its top. A short target (up to 1.5 m) that the cut would pass through is cut 0.05 m above its top, so it shows whole. Taller targets (walls, columns) are cut at 1.2 m like a plan drawing. The storey is the one of the target element; without storeys it is the highest level at or below the target centre, else the target base. The view frames the target at 4 times its larger side, at least 6 m across.
- **Section**: an orthographic vertical cut through the target centroid. The cut is perpendicular to the horizontal part of the surface normal when that is clearly horizontal (within 30 degrees of the horizontal plane; the view looks into the surface), else perpendicular to the longer horizontal side of the target (the view looks along it, from the side of the authored camera). A back plane `max(2 m, 2 x the target depth)` behind the cut keeps the depth short. The view frames the target at 3 times its larger across or height, at least 5 m.

What the target is: the stroke of a surface or element drawing on the model as it lies now, the end points of a measurement, the overlap of a penetration's pipe and host (else both), the region box, the anchor of a text box. With a drawing selected, the live views use that drawing; otherwise the union of the tied drawings. Everything is derived from the data: nothing is stored on the view.

Plan and Section use the pseudo-orthographic projection of the viewer (2 degree field of view, the camera moved back, as the `P` key does) and `renderer.setClipPlanes` with caps, the way a BCF viewpoint applies its planes. The attached drawings follow, because an attachment projects in any camera. A section you had (the section tool's box or plane, or the planes of an applied BCF viewpoint) is remembered when you leave 3D for the first time and put back when you press 3D. Planes that someone sets after the switch (you move the section tool) stay. Leaving the orthographic projection by hand (`P`, a saved view) ends the switch as the control shows it. The view switch is one camera move in the global undo stack (the camera watch records it); the clip planes are not on that stack. Pure derivation: `src/issues/derivedViews.ts`; viewer side: `src/issues/derivedSwitch.ts`; control: `src/issues/viewSwitchUi.ts`. Scripting: `__xrayIssues.viewMode(id)`, `setViewMode(id, mode)`, `viewSwitchAvailable(id)`, `derivedView(id, 'plan' | 'section')`.

**BCF export.** Every export (and so every issue raised from a tied view and exported) also writes the Plan and the Section of each tied view as two more viewpoints of the topic, right after the view's viewpoint: an `OrthogonalCamera` with `ViewToWorldScale` (metres of visible height) and `AspectRatio` (3.0), `ClippingPlanes` (`Direction` points into the cut-away half), and the Selection, Visibility, Coloring and 3D `Lines` of the source viewpoint. They have no snapshot. Other BCF tools read them as ordinary orthographic viewpoints with clipping planes. They are made at export from the current view, not stored in the session, so an edited view always exports fresh views. The side file marks each one: `"derived": { "kind": "plan" | "section", "sketch": "<sketch id>", "of": "<viewpoint guid>" }`. Opening the file makes the view of the source viewpoint only: a derived viewpoint is a view of that view and never gets a view (`sketchFromViewpoint` refuses it). The issue detail lists it as `Plan (derived)` or `Section (derived)` with `Apply` and `Clear`. Exporting again replaces the derived viewpoints (their guids stay) and never doubles them; derived viewpoints of a file whose source has no tied view here are written back as they are.

## The drawing toolbar

A small floating bar, shown only while a view or an issue's viewpoint is edited (`window.__xrayMarkup.editing`; `Edit drawings`, `Draw on this view` or choosing a tool in a script start it, `Done` ends it). It has all the tools of the markup tool (select, cloud, cloud draw, callout, text, pointer, marker, highlight box, rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow, polyline, polygon, pen), the colours, the line weights, `Fill`, the text of the next callout, `Undo` and `Redo` of the view's markup history, `Finish` for a polyline in progress and `Done`. When the active view is tied to a 3D construct the bar has one more row: `3D · Plan · Section` (see "Plan and Section views"). It sits bottom centre inside the edge chrome and moves aside or up where another reserved rect is (the gizmo, legends), through the shared overlay reserve API (`ov.reserve('markup-float', ...)`, `toolbarSpot` in `src/markup/toolbar.ts`); it never covers the gizmo or the top bar, and wraps to fit 390 px.

The global undo stack skips while the toolbar is active (`markupHasKeys` in `src/plugins/undo.ts` checks `editing`). Pointer modes Arrow and Hand keep working: a drawing tool takes the left button, other buttons still orbit and pan. Keys while editing: `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Shift+Z`, `Delete`, `Escape` (cancel the drawing, then back to select, then deselect).

## Kept between visits

3D views, issues and the links between them are saved in **IndexedDB** (database `xray-work`, `src/work/`) and come back when the same model loads. One record per model, keyed the way `markup/scope.ts` groups items (`identityKey`: name, element count, GlobalId hash), so a view made on one model is never restored on another. A record holds the 3D views, the issues (BCF topics with their viewpoints, comments and pins), the snapshot images (as bytes, in the same record) and the viewpoint-to-view links (`sketchId`). The PDFs an issue refers to are saved as metadata only; their bytes stay in the sheets store and are found again by SHA-256. Sheet views are kept by the sheets store, as before.

- Saves follow changes after a quiet moment (800 ms), skip writes that change nothing, and run once more when the page is hidden. Nothing is written before the saved items of the loaded model have been read back, so a reload cannot wipe them.
- On a model load the plugin (`src/plugins/work.ts`, after Sheets) puts back the views and issues of that model: sets first, then topics, so the links are whole. A status line says how many came back.
- `Clear all drawings and issues for this model` (bottom of the Issues tab and of the drawings part of the Views tab, two clicks, the second names the counts) removes the drawings of the 3D views and the issues of the loaded model from the session and from the saved copy. Items of other models and sheet views stay. Opening a BCF file without merge still replaces the issues of the session, and the saved copy follows.
- None of it is in URL parameters, share links, saved views, presets or packed pages, and nothing is in localStorage (`tests/work-store.test.ts` scans the code, as `sheets-store.test.ts` does). Without IndexedDB (a private window) nothing is kept and the session works as before.
- Scripting: `window.__xrayWork` = `{ save(), restored(), counts(), clear(), wipe(), persistent, status }`.

## Make issue, and how the link works

`Make issue` (row menu and detail of a view in the Views tab, with the chooser `New issue` / `Add to: <issue>`; `window.__xrayViews.makeIssue(id, { topic? })`; `window.__xrayIssues.raise(setId, { topic? })`, or `window.__xrayMarkup.toBcf(setId)`): the view is restored, the issue is made from the title and note (status `Open`), the viewpoint captures the camera, selection, hides, colours, clip planes and a snapshot, and the viewpoint gets `sketchId`; the view gets `issue: { topic, viewpoint }`. With `topic` the view is added to that issue as one more viewpoint.

- **One view, at most one issue.** Raising a view that already belongs to an issue is refused (`linkSketch` in `src/issues/link.ts`); open that issue instead.
- **Not a copy.** The viewpoint's drawing (`annotations`) is a projection of the view, rebuilt by `syncViewpoint` after every view change and again before every export. Shape points are fractions of the view camera and the viewpoint's snapshot camera is set to that same camera, so they copy over without projecting; clouds are closed, ellipses become polygons and arrows get a head. Elements that the drawing points at join the viewpoint selection (other BCF tools highlight them), as before.
- **Editing updates the issue.** Draw, move, delete, retype, undo or change an attachment: the issue's drawing follows, and `composeSnapshot` and the BCF export burn and serialise the current drawing. `Update view` on a view captures the issue viewpoint again from the same live view. While a viewpoint belongs to a view the BCF overlay draws no annotations of it (the markup view draws the view), so nothing is drawn twice.
- **Removing a view that belongs to an issue asks first** (a confirmation box in the panel, `removeSketch(id)` returns `confirm` until called with `confirmed`). The issue keeps the camera view and loses that drawing. Deleting an issue leaves its views as loose views.
- Links are repaired when topics change (`reconcileLinks`): the viewpoint's `sketchId` is the truth, `set.issue` the mirror.

## Export report (PDF and CSV)

The issues list has a collapsed block `Export report` (`src/issues/reportUi.ts`) with a **page size** (A4 or Letter, portrait) and **Plan/Section images** (both on by default) and two buttons. The report is of the list as it is filtered now: the status chip (All, Open, Closed), the search and the loaded model (issues of other models are left out). The cover says which filter it was made with. Scripts: `window.__xrayIssueReport = { pdf({ page?, planSection?, download? }), csv({ download? }), model(), options, setOptions }`.

**PDF** (`PDF report (n)`; built with the hand-written writer `src/markup/pdf.ts`, which gained links to a page and bookmarks; no dependency):

- **Cover** (page 1, and the pages after it when the list is long): model name, project name, date and author, the filter, counts (total, open, closed and a bar per status) and a table of the issues (number, title, status, priority, assignee, due date, page). Each row is a link to the issue's page.
- **One page per issue**, with a link back to the cover and bookmarks for the cover and each issue: title, status, priority, type, assignee, due date, labels, created and last changed (with the authors), the description, the **views** (the snapshot with its drawings burned in, as the detail shows it; for a sheet issue the sheet name, its PDF and page and the **region image**), the **target elements** (name, IFC class, GlobalId; the first 24, then a count), and the **comments with their author names and dates**. An issue with a long thread continues on further pages ("continued"), so "one page" holds for the usual issue only. The footer says `Page n of N`.
- **Plan and Section**: for an issue whose view is tied to the model (the same condition as the 3D, Plan, Section switch; sheet issues have none) a small Plan and Section sit beside the snapshot. They are rendered on the live canvas through `DerivedViewSwitch` (`src/issues/derivedSwitch.ts`, `reportRender.ts`): the issue viewpoint is applied, the switch is put on Plan and on Section and the canvas is read in the same task. A still copy of the canvas is laid over it for the whole run, so the view does not flicker, and the camera, section planes, hides and look you had are put back at the end (`window.__xrayViews` capture and restore). With the option off, no view is switched at all.
- Pictures are re-encoded as RGB JPEG (snapshots up to 1100 px, Plan and Section up to 640 px) and embedded once each.

**CSV** (`CSV (n)`): **one file, one row per issue, with a `Comments` column** (every comment as `author (date): text`, one per line inside the cell) and a `Comment count`. A second file of comments would have to be joined by hand to be of use, and a spreadsheet opens one file as it is. Columns: Number, GUID, Title, Status, Priority, Type, Assigned to, Due date, Labels, Created, Created by, Modified, Modified by, Description, Comment count, Comments, Elements (names), GlobalIds, Sheet, Sheet page, Raised from. RFC 4180 quoting, CRLF, UTF-8 with a byte order mark so a spreadsheet reads the umlauts; text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets a leading apostrophe (the formula guard of `src/tools/csv.ts`).

The comments carry author names because the person exports the file on purpose. File names: `xray-issues-<model>-<yyyymmdd-hhmm>.pdf` and `.csv`.

## Issues from checks (penetrations and clearance)

Both drawers have an `Issues` block with **Raise issues from results** (`src/issues/clashUi.ts`; the logic is in `src/issues/clash.ts` (pure) and `src/issues/clashRaise.ts`). Each result becomes an issue:

- **Title**: `Penetration: Duct-12 through Wall-3`, or `Clearance 120 mm < 300 mm: Pipe-4 / Beam-9` (the distance and the `tight (m)` limit of the run). Type `Clash`, labels `clash` and `penetration` or `clearance`, priority from the severity (penetration: no opening or an opening too small is High, unknown is Normal, a fitting opening is Low; clearance: touching is High).
- **Components**: both GlobalIds are the selection of the viewpoint. The camera frames the pair (for a long pipe beside a long beam: where they are close, `pairRegion`), with a pin and a snapshot.
- **A view tied to the pair**: a rectangle around the region on the viewpoint's view, with the attachment kind `penetration` (the MEP element and the host) for penetrations and `measurement` (a link with both GlobalIds and the distance) for clearance. It is tied, so the issue gets the 3D, Plan, Section switch and a Plan and Section in the report.
- **Which rows**: penetrations: the selected rows, else the filtered rows of the table; clearance: every clashing or tight pair of the last run (the clearance colouring must be on, with a focus).

**No doubles.** An issue is found again by **the check type plus the sorted GlobalId pair** (`clashKey`), stored on the topic as `clash` in the BCF side file (`xray-markup.json`, optional field `clash: { check, key, a, b, missing? }`), so it survives a BCF export and open. Raising after a re-run raises only the results without an issue. The block shows the counts: **new / already raised / not found in the latest run**.

**Results that no longer occur** are never deleted. An issue of the same check whose pair the latest run does not report (and that the run could have found: the pair must have been in the MEP set and the hosts it analysed, or in the focus and context it looked at; another focus or scope says nothing about it) gets one comment, `Not found in latest run`, and `clash.missing`. `Close n not found` sets the status of those that are still open to Closed. A pair that comes back gets `Found again in the latest run`.

**Batch limit.** Above **200** new results the block asks first: `Raise top 200 by severity` (penetrations: no opening, then an opening too small, then unknown, then a fitting opening, larger sizes first; clearance: the smallest distance first), `Raise all n`, or Cancel. The work runs one issue at a time with a frame between them, shows `Raising issues d / n` and has a `Stop` button, and the issues panel does not jump into the detail of each new issue (`IssuesController.batch`). Scripts: `window.__xrayClash = { config: { maxBatch }, preview(check), raise(check, { limit? }), close(check) }`; `preview` returns `{ fresh, already, resolved }`.

## BCF import

Opening a BCF file keeps working as before (2.0, 2.1, 3.0; the side file, Lines and Components). Every viewpoint with annotations and a camera that can carry shapes becomes a **view linked to its issue** (`sketchFromViewpoint`): the markups are made from the annotations (callout, note, arrow, measurement callout, rectangle, freehand line as pen), numbered in order, the view camera is the snapshot camera, the view holds only that camera, and restoring the view applies the BCF viewpoint (`fromViewpoint`). A viewpoint whose drawing cannot become markup (a foreign line set without a shape) keeps its drawing as it is, drawn by the BCF overlay, and gets no view. The same happens to annotations added through `window.__xrayBcf.addAnnotation` and `addShape` on a viewpoint that has none yet; on a viewpoint that already is a view these calls (and `updateAnnotation`, `removeAnnotation`) go through the view (`BcfController.linked`), so a script never loses an annotation to the next projection. Until the view changes, the drawing of an imported viewpoint stays exactly as the file had it (no relabelling). The session link (`sketchId`, `issue`) is not written to the BCF file; the file stores the derived annotations in the side file, so re-opening an export makes the views again.

## Migration of old markup sets

Old sets kept review data on their markups: a comment thread, a resolved status, an assignee, a due date. Views have none of these any more, and nothing is lost:

- Sets with comments or a non-default status show a banner in the Views tab: **"N views have comments or a status. Make issues of them?"** (singular: "1 view has comments or a status. Make an issue of it?"). `Yes, make issues` converts each set into an issue that links to the view (`migrateLegacy`): title of the view, the note (or a generated line) as description, status `Open` when any markup was open and `Resolved` otherwise, the first assignee, the earliest due date, and every comment as an issue comment (`"<markup number>. <text>"`, original author and date, oldest first). The comments and status are then removed from the view. `Not now` hides the banner for the session.
- Until converted, the legacy comments and status stay in the data and show read-only on the view ("Earlier comments and status") and in search.
- Opening an old markup JSON (or a PDF that carries one) works as before and shows the same banner.
- `window.__xrayMarkup.comment` and the status, assignee and due fields of `update` and `add` no longer change a view (`comment` returns false); the data of old documents is still read and written.

## Search and status bar

The top-bar search has a section **Issues** ([search.md](search.md)): title, description and comment text, every word must match, a title hit ranks above a description hit above a comment hit; the row says which field matched. Enter opens the issue and brings its view back. Views have their own section **Views** (name, note, issue title). `searchIssues` in `src/issues/search.ts` is pure.

The status bar shows `N open issues` (issues of the loaded model whose status is not resolved, closed, done, solved, completed, rejected, cancelled, fixed or verified; `isOpenStatus`). Clicking it opens the Issues tab with the `Open` chip.

## Keys, commands and ids

`K` (action `markup.toggle`) opens the Views tab on the filter `With drawings`. `tool.markup` and `tool.bcf` are kept as aliases (Views tab on `With drawings`, Issues tab); `markup.new`, `markup.open`, `markup.pdf`, `markup.json`, `bcf.open`, `bcf.create`, `bcf.export`, `bcf.clear` work as before (the ones that used to need a drawer open the tab first). New: `issues.open`, `issues.sketches` (the Views tab on `With drawings`), `issues.edit` (toolbar for the active view), `issues.done`. A packaged page whose boot config says `openTool: "bcf"` or `"markup"` (or lists them in `tools`) opens the tab.

## File formats

Additive only. The markup document stays `xray-markup-set/1`: a set may carry `note`, `issue` (`{ topic, viewpoint }`) and `fromViewpoint`; older readers ignore them and old documents read as before (comments, status, assignee, due keep reading and writing). The BCF side file gained the optional per-viewpoint `derived` marker (above); older readers ignore it. The PDF report and its attached JSON are unchanged (the table still lists legacy status and comments of a set that has them). Names, e-mail addresses and pictures of people stay out of params, share links, saved views, presets and packed pages (the leak test); the author text of views and issues lives in the markup JSON and the BCF file only, as before. The side file also gained an optional topic field `clash` (`{ check, key, a, b, missing? }`: the check result an issue was raised from, see "Issues from checks"); a topic with only a `clash` still gets a side file.
