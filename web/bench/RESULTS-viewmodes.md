## Reading these numbers

The machine was heavily loaded by other jobs while this ran (load average 10 to 13 on a few cores), and swiftshader renders on those same cores:
the first and the last row are the same configuration (view modes off) and differ by a factor of two. Only a rough ordering is
meaningful, and the frame count per row (5 to 17) is small. Observations that do hold up:

- Peel mask rebuilds: 0 with a still camera (the mask, the flood and the 10 passes are skipped when camera, viewport, focus and hidden flags are unchanged), one per frame while orbiting.
- Frame CPU (JS time) stays at 1 to 3 ms with the modes on and still; the work of the modes is on the rasteriser side, which swiftshader cannot price for a GPU.
- Peel while orbiting costs a focus-only draw of the merged geometry plus 10 full-screen passes at half resolution each frame: it is the expensive case, comparable to the bloom mask render; on a GPU these are tiny full-screen passes.
