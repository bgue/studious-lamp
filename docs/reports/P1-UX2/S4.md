## Learnings (proposed)
The brief (SCOPE section 4, README D5) says textual-image is MIT. It is LGPL-3.0-or-later on PyPI (0.14.2 and the older releases checked), so the stated licence assumption was wrong and the owner decision 2026-10-21 (APPROVALS b57c20b) rests on it.

## Open questions / Blocked
Blocked: textual-image is LGPL-3.0-or-later and `check_licences.py` refuses it; the spec says to stop and change nothing else. Decision needed from the owner/supervisor:
1. Replace textual-image with a permissive route. Options: drive the terminal image protocols directly (kitty graphics, iTerm2 inline images, sixel) in a small module of our own on top of Pillow (MIT-CMU) and Rich, with no new dependency beyond pypdfium2; or find another MIT/BSD library.
2. Or the owner accepts LGPL for this one dependency (an ADR-0006 exception, and a `check_licences.py` allow-list entry, which is outside S4's allowed paths).
3. Or ship the text fallback only (page count, size, title) and drop the thumbnail for now. pypdfium2 would still be used for the page count and the cached PNG.
Everything else in the spec (temp, cache, viewer, render/_worker, FilePanel, document log on LogScreen, Files tab) is unaffected by the choice, except `FilePanel`'s thumbnail branch.
Note for the re-spec: the dev scripts `dev/demos/p1_i2_demo.py`, `p1_i2b_demo.py` and `dev/tour/phase1.py` look up `#docs-table` and `#docs-status`; moving the register to LogScreen renames these to `#log-table` and `#log-status`, and those files are outside S4's allowed paths (S6 or a widened S4).
