## Learnings (proposed)
- `ClientInterface` names collide with existing readers of the same noun (`get_look_ahead` belonged to the schedule). Check names when the ticket is written.
- Fake attributes share one namespace across `FakeClient` mixins and test subclasses. pyright reports `sent`-style clashes only at `just check`; prefix new attribute names (`_awp_*`).
