## Learnings (proposed)

- The confidentiality walk turns any new required tool argument into a failure until it has candidate values. That is the intended fail-closed behaviour, but the implementer must know to extend `values()`.
- The awp `WeekWorld` helpers (`progress_world.py`) are reachable from tl-mcp tests by path, as `test_awp_tools.py` does for `awp_world.py`.
