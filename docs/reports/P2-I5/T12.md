## Learnings (proposed)
- `tl constraint raise --phase` must name a guard phase; for an IWP the phase of an unnamed guard is the transition name (`start`), not `issued`.

- `awp iwp release-hold` moves to Constraint check, not Issued; `week commit` needs `--hours`.
