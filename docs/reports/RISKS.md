# Risk register after Phase 0

Purpose: the brief §17 register brought up to date with what Phase 0 showed, plus the risks the build run itself found. Written 2026-10-11 by the P0-I8 workstream E supervisor. Evidence is a report, learning (`L-…` in `docs/memory/LEARNINGS.md`), ADR or file. Numbers marked *pending D* are completed from `docs/reports/P0-I8-perf.md` when workstream D merges.

Scales: likelihood and impact are L (low), M (medium) or H (high) as judged for a first customer project. Owner is **human** (a decision or action only the owner can take; most are `04-gates.md` §2 gates) or **agent** (the build agents can do the work). Status: open, mitigated (a control exists and is tested), monitoring, accepted (a decision to live with it), not started (the area has not been built).

## 3. Reading the register

The risks that should decide the next phase's ordering, in order:
1. X11 and Q5/Q6 (no permission model) and X10 (secrets at rest): everything customer-facing waits on them. Both are the owner's.
2. R12 and X15 (no schema classification, no upgrade path): the first schema change that matters will find the gap.
3. X7 and X8 (Postgres only in tests; one write lock): the production data store is the least exercised path.
4. R10, X3 and X4 (agent-written code): the controls work and are expensive; keep them.
5. X12 (CI never ran): cheap to close, and it is the only gate that is not observed.
