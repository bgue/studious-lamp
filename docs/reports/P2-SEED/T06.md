## What it builds (`constraints` section, needs `core.Constraint` and `awp.IWP`)
- 24 IWPs on Released CWPs, each taken Draft, In development, Constraint check (awp handlers, as the foreman-planner).
- 45 constraints, raised by `RaiseConstraint` (manual, owner an existing project person), moved by `AssignConstraint`,
  `ClearConstraint`, `WithdrawConstraint`: 33 on 20 IWPs, 12 on 12 CWPs (blocking `start_execution` or advisory).
- One check-sourced constraint (`materials_reserved`, raised through `tl_core.constraints.raise_constraint` as awp)
  cleared by a person with `ClearConstraint` (no waive word exists; none added).
- Readiness: every IWP evaluated through `tl_mod_awp.readiness.evaluate_now` on 2026-11-06, so the ledger holds
  `IWP.ReadinessEvaluated` and the Constraint check to Ready moves.
- Dates: `due_at` and `cleared_at` are world time on the constraint; `effective_at` is never set (ADR-0024 D19).
  Envelope time is `world.on(day)` only. Draws from the section rng only.
