## Learnings (proposed)
- `documents.ProcessingJob` keys are `JOB-` + `new_ulid()` (`derivation/derived.py`, `_queue_job`). Any section that runs an extraction breaks the key-set determinism unless the test excludes that type.
- `AssignMtoLines` writes `cur_materials_mto_line.work_package_id`; the awp CWP and IWP keys are the `awp.CWP` and `awp.IWP` record types.
- The ticket's "real shortages (required > reserved)" is satisfied by almost every line (149 of 150 here), because reservations are few. A stricter shortage check (required > stock on hand) would be a better test; I did not change it.
- `DocumentsWorld` and `World` differ: `World.upload` has no `by_module`, so the MTR slot needs the file service with `by_module=True`.
