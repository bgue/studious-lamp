## Learnings (proposed)
- `tl_mod_correspondence.testing.at(day)` moves the module clock and the ledger clock together; usable for any seed or tour.
- Issuing a letter that `responds_to` an item already marks that item Responded; `RecordResponse` afterwards is refused.
- A clock needs `<project>-default@<n>.yaml` in the schema dir; a new project needs its calendar fixture.
