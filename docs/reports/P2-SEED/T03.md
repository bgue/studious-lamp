## Learnings (proposed)
- `dev.seed.demo_project.world.PROJECT` is the scope `project:P200`, not the id. Screens and `build_app` take `PROJECT_ID`.
- Log screens never show a `Showing` line when empty; the screen reads `N of M ...`. Waiting on `len(app.screen.rows)` is more reliable.
- `leaks_in` flags version suffixes like `@0` and `@1`, which appear in MTO numbers (`…/MTO/001`) and the receipt lot `P200-RCPT-0001@1`.

## Open questions / Blocked

These are the MTO line numbers (`P200-200-ISO-0901@0/MTO/001`, 23 on the take-off screen) and the lot reference `P200-RCPT-0001@1` on the stock panel. The tour exits 1 and the gate fails. The options are:
1. Treat these as product references and allow them in plain shots (a change to the leak rule, tourkit or `tl_tui.display`, which is outside this ticket).
2. Mark the take-off and stock shots `technical=True` (the check is skipped). This weakens the check, so I did not do it.
3. Pick other screens or filters so the shots avoid `@N` rows.
Please decide. I have not changed the leak rule.
