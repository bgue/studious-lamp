## Learnings (proposed)
- `world.PROJECT` is the scope `project:P200`, not the id. Screens and `build_app` take `PROJECT_ID`.
- Empty log screens show no `Showing` line; they read `N of M ...`. Wait on `len(app.screen.rows)`.
- `leaks_in` flags `@N` suffixes in MTO line numbers (`P200-200-ISO-0901@0/MTO/001`) and the lot
  `P200-RCPT-0001@1`. The check cannot tell product references from technical ids, so `technical=True` is a
  whole-shot exemption; a per-token allowance would be narrower.
- `just check` runs past the 120 s agent-shell timeout; run it in the background and poll the log.
