## Deviations from plan
- Events reach the app with `post_message`, not `call_from_thread` (decision D1); the grid's worker applies its result with `call_from_thread` and a generation guard.
- Open record views re-read on the UI thread (D11); filter, load and reload calls in remote mode also run on the UI thread with a 3 s timeout (D16). Moving them to workers is the P0-I8 hardening follow-up.
- `tl-tui` depends on `tl-api` and `httpx2` (BSD-3-Clause, already locked), `tl-cli` on `tl-tui` (workspace edges). No new third-party licence.
- The filter does not restrict the record type (O3 has no `record_type`); say `type:...` in the text (D8).

## Escalations and decisions
- Fresh review of S20 to S22 at 41c938a: changes requested, two medium and four low findings, all fixed in S23 with tests that force the order and fail without the fix: the feed cursor is taken before the first read (no gap); events of an in-flight command are held until the response is noted, with a 2 s cap; a server whose head is below the cursor resets the feed, and the app reads everything again with a banner; marks are kept until applied, an unexpected refresh error is reported and resets the busy flag; the interactive timeout is 3 s; the `--token` help says to prefer `TL_TOKEN`.
- Orchestrator ruling on the flaky outage test: root-cause it, no skipping or loosening. Cause: the row can appear through the read after "connection restored" and the mark through the replayed event a moment later; the assertion fired at the first of the two. The test now waits for the mark (the claim is that both orders end marked). 30 of 30 and 20 of 20 repeats pass.
- No human gate was touched; real authentication remains a human gate (ADR-0005). No core (03 §7) interface changed.
