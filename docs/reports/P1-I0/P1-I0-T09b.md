## Deviations
Two small deviations from the literal STEPS, both to satisfy the ticket's general rule that the password inputs are emptied "after **every** attempt that reached a check or a call" (Specification):
- `AccountForm.action_apply`: the "Enter a username" branch also calls `self._clear_passwords()` before returning (STEPS says only `self._error(...)` and return). Without it, a password typed before the username was forgotten would stay in the masked input.
- `PasswordForm.action_apply`: the "Enter the new password" branch also calls `self._clear_passwords()` before returning, for the same reason (the repeat field may hold text).

Nothing else changed outside the STEPS bodies. The base `_Form.action_apply` in `account_forms.py` still raises `NotImplementedError`: it has no STEPS comment, it is the abstract hook, and all three forms override it. I left it as the stub had it.

## Learnings (proposed)
- The ticket's "remove every STEPS comment" leaves the base `_Form.action_apply` raising `NotImplementedError` (no STEPS comment on it). A reviewer grepping for `NotImplementedError` will find it; it is intentional, but the ticket could say so.
- The Specification's "emptied after every attempt that reached a check" conflicts with the literal STEPS for the empty-username and empty-password branches (STEPS omit the clear there). The spec wording was followed; the STEPS should be aligned.

## Open questions / Blocked
- `int()` in `TokenForm` accepts forms such as `"+7"`, `" 7 "` (stripped) and `"1_0"` (= 10). The STEPS say `int(...)`; I did not add a stricter check because the ticket says the forms check only "that the days are a whole number" and the service owns the rest. Confirm whether `1_0` should be rejected by the form.
- Negative days (for example `-3`) are passed to the service unchanged; the form does not check them. Whether the service refuses them was not tested here.
