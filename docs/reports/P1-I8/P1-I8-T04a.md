## Learnings (proposed)
- The STEPS for `property_lines` say `<enforcement>` without saying where it comes from. The fixtures need the pset fallback (`prop.enforcement or pset.enforcement`); a stub docstring that says so would save a red run.
- The `custom` key in `extends` has no `x.` prefix in the YAML; the docstring's `x.<name>` is a literal format, which the stub does not say.
- The ticket names `/tmp/pt-<ticket>` for basetemp; the environment rule points temp files at the scratchpad. The ticket and the environment disagree here.
