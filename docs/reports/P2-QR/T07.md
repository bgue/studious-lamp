## Learnings (proposed)
- `qr_world.record` sets `title=key`, so the default label text (`label_fields(...).text`) prints the record key as a `<div class="text">` line. A record titled with its key therefore puts the key in the label file, which the ticket's rule forbids.
- In the QR test ledger, `user` and `document_controller` both hold `qr.view`, and an account needs at least one role. So no roled account lacks the view word; the "no view word" miss is simulated by patching `resolve.view_word_held`.
