## Learnings (proposed)
- The shared fake (`tl_sim.testing.FakeClient.accept_proposal`) diverges from the API: it returns no `result_stream_id` and keeps accepted CWPs in `cwp_rows`, not `record_rows`, so `query("id:...")` cannot see them. Any test relying on the fake for an accepted record will silently get no RECORD_CREATED. A supervisor ticket should make the fake match the API.
