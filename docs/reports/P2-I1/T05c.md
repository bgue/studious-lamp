## Learnings (proposed)
- The reason of a path version is stored on its steps (`path.yaml` `reason_code`, "for the re-sequencing that made this version"), and `PathVersion` has no reason field, so a reason per version needs a read of that version's steps. The ticket's Context does not mention either fact.
- The ticket's `path_page` contract takes no previous-version view, but the state line's "reason of the first changed step" needs one.
