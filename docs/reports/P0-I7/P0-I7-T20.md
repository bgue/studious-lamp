## Learnings (proposed)
- `tl lake status` after `rebuild --yes` reports `syncs 1`: a rebuild resets the sync count. The provided test asserts this; the spec text does not say it. Worth a line in the `tl_lake` docs.
- The `truncated at <limit> rows` line uses `result.limit` (the limit the service applied), not the raw `--limit` value. They are equal whenever `--limit` is valid, and the service refuses invalid values before the CLI prints.
