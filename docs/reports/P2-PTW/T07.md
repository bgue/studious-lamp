## Learnings (proposed)
- Every account role (`admin`, `document_controller`, `user`) derives `read` on every module, and every grant role (`viewer`, `user`, `superuser`) includes `read`. `permits.view` maps to `read` (`schema/settings/access-words.yaml`). So an enabled account always holds `permits.view` on any project. A disabled account gets 401 (not 403) on the API.
- `python -m tl_api.openapi` with no flag writes the file; the raw `git diff` looks large (alignment), so compare paths and schemas.
