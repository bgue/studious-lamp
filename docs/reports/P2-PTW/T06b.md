## Learnings (proposed)
- A hot-work or confined-space permit cannot be issued without a gas test no older than `gas_valid_minutes` (`gas_test_stale`), and `gas_test_missing` otherwise. Test helpers must seed the gas test at the world clock, then issue.
- Output says `P123-GAS-…` keys; the gas-test export header names the key `number`, not `key`.

## Open questions / Blocked
**Blocked, needs a decision:** should `tl gastest ls` on a hidden permit print `no gas tests` (ticket Tests item 3) or `error: no permit <KEY>`? The ticket also requires an unknown key to print the error, so the two cannot both hold without a lookup that sees hidden records (AGENTS §49 forbids that). I implemented and tested the error answer. If the ticket text stands, a supervisor must decide how to answer a hidden key that does not reveal it is hidden.
