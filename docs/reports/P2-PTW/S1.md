## Deviations
Four, all small. (1) The ten events that carry no world time of their own (`Requested`, `Approved`, `Rejected`,
`Suspended`, `HandedBack`, `Closed`, `Cancelled`, `Lapsed`, `IsolationLinked`, `IsolationUnlinked`) got an optional `at`
tagged `tl:valid_time`, the P2-PUNCH precedent (7c4ebaa9), instead of lines in `tests/tools/valid_time_baseline.toml`:
`test_valid_time_tag.py::test_baseline_never_grows` pins the baseline to a frozen set that may not grow, and that file is
not in Allowed paths. The baseline file is unchanged. (2) `compat/event-schemas.json` also gained 10 awp events that the
trunk baseline lacked (`Cwp.PartyAssigned`, `IWP.*`, `WeeklyPlan.*`, ...): `just compat-update` writes the whole schema;
nothing existing changed. (3) Gas readings in `GasTest.Recorded` are decimal text (as materials `qty`), bound as float.
(4) Workflow ids are `permits.permit` and `permits.isolation`. `feed/cards.py` was not touched (no test refused the events).
