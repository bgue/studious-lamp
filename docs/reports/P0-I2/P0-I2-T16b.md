## Learnings (proposed)
- A `StatusMessage` posted by the modal right before `dismiss(True)` still reaches the app
  (`on_status_message` on the app saw it in `test_a_partial_save_dismisses_true_and_reports_what_was_applied`).
  This is the case the ticket relies on, and it held without any extra routing.
- `FieldEditor` and `pset_value` both accept the `psets.` prefix on field paths, so the form passes
  `field.path` straight through as specified.

## Open questions / Blocked
- Not blocking, for the supervisor to decide: `save_record_edits` raises `ValueError` (from
  `pset_batches`) for a non-readonly pset field whose layer is `enrichment` or `source`. `EditForm`
  lists every non-readonly field, as the ticket specifies, and does not catch that `ValueError`, so
  such a field would surface as an exception from `action_save` rather than in the form. No fake
  metadata in the provided tests exercises this combination, so I left the behaviour as specified.
  If the metadata can produce it, a follow-up ticket should decide whether the form filters those
  fields or shows the error.
