## Learnings (proposed)
- `test_link_projector.py::test_ddl_creates_both_tables` has `@pytest.mark.parametrize("dialect", ...)`, which shadows the `dialect` fixture. Pytest lets the direct parametrize win, so the test stays at two items and does not touch the adapter. This is correct, but a reader who expects the parity fixture there will be misled.
- The ticket's "every test keeps its name" conflicts with its own rename directive for the DDL test. A future ticket that renames a test should say so in *Tests to add*.
- `just test-parity` prints `dev up: skipped MinIO (no Docker daemon); the fs object store is used instead` before the run. It is harmless and does not affect the result.
- The parity total (104) is lower than the `--adapters sqlite,postgres` total (125) by design: pure tests carry no adapter marker. The ticket's note already says so; the count explanation is worth keeping next to the table.
