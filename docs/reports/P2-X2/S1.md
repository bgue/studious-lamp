## What it does
`agent_scope.check_filing` reads `module_roles.effective_roles(...)` and accepts only a `source` of `granted`,
`granted_all_modules` or `admin`. Module command: `user` must be in `words`; the command's own word is not asked. Core command:
any module (or `*`) with such a source and at least one role on the scope. A role derived from the account role never counts.
Refusal is always `RecordNotFoundError("no scope '<id>'")` (API 404): no module, word or role shows. Wiring in `proposals.py` unchanged.

## Learnings (proposed)
`holder_for_actor` gives an account-less agent nothing, and `derive` gives any user-role account every module's user word, so
filing needs both an account and an explicit grant in a fixture. A grant adds events: tests that count events should filter.
