## Item 6 (events visibility)
The events carry `agent` (an actor string) and an optional reason of at most 200 characters: they cannot carry record text. They sit on
`core.Account`, already in `INTERNAL_STREAM_TYPES`, so `/events`, `/stream` and the change feed hide them from non-admins today; no new
`hide_stream_types` entry is needed.

## Learnings (proposed)
Events on the account stream must move `cur_account.version`; a projector other than `AccountProjector` does it here. Rebuilding only the `accounts`
projector leaves the version stale until a full rebuild.
