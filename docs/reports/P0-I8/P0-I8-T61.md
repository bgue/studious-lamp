## Learnings (proposed)
- `_update_promoted` runs a column-introspection query (`SELECT * FROM cur_core_record WHERE 1 = 0`) on every call, even when no promoted columns exist. A create therefore costs 2 statements, not the 1 the insert alone suggests. The budget of 3 holds with that in mind; a future budget of 1 would need the introspection cached or skipped.
- For scratch scripts against Postgres, `sqlalchemy.create_engine("postgresql://...")` fails with `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'psycopg'` in this environment. Use `tl_adapters.db.make_engine(url)`, as the conftest `new_engine` fixture does.
- Ticket sketch and parenthetical disagreed on the bound-parameter naming (`:c_0` versus `record_id_0`). Naming it in the ticket would avoid a judgement call.

## Open questions / Blocked
- None blocking. Two points for the supervisor to confirm:

I did not read the `cur_pset_values` DDL to check for a unique key on `(record_id, path)`, since it is outside the ticket's context list.
