## Learnings (proposed)
- The P2-I4 demo ledger defines no locations (`tl init --dev` plus the loads). Any clause that needs a toolcrib must define TC1 first, since `ToolcribScreen` is ready only when `list_materials_locations` lists TC1 as a toolcrib.
- The T06 ticket text says the driver creates TC1 "the way T06.py does"; T06.py does not create locations, it relies on the seed `.accept/p2-i3.db`.
- The account role `user` derives `materials user` (`schema/settings/module-roles.yaml`), so `uma` needed no grant.
- A demo clause prints its own `PASS clause N` lines from the driver and a second one from the shell, as clauses 5 and 6 already do.
- Stale text in `run_backend` (`echo "[$backend] clauses 1-4 passed"`) was left alone (outside the ticket).
