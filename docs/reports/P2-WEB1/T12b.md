## Learnings (proposed)
- The base `WebLog.keep_columns` default assumes a `State` column. A log without one (movements) must override it to keep its own always-shown columns, or a constant column that the terminal keeps gets dropped on the web.
- The fake's `list_materials_movements` already takes `since`/`until`/`lot`/`limit`, so no fake change was needed.
