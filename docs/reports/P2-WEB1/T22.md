## Design choices (from the spec, stated here)
- The primary look applies to `main button[type="submit"]`, not to every submit button. The header's Sign out has no class yet, so `header.tl-bar button` gets the ghost `.tl-btn` look until S7 adds the class.
- Tone classes set the text colour (`.tl-done` etc.); `.tl-badge.tl-<tone>` adds the weak background. A mini-table cell with a tone gets coloured text, not a pill.
- `kbd` inside a primary button is white on a translucent white, for contrast.
- `.tl-crumb` is `nowrap` with ellipsis, so the crumb stays one line.
