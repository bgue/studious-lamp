## tui-layout checklist (skill has nine lines; ticket asked for seven)
1. Glance answers where / what / state / next: the tile heading and summary line; the list is the rows.
2. Identity line first: the view is named in the panel heading; no record identity (an aggregate tile).
3. Sections: heading plus summary in one block; no sections needed.
4. Tiers: everyday values only (weeks, hours, bounds); no ids.
5. Log columns: the backlog view starts with Foreman, not State; no sideways scroll at 170 (`mount 170`).
6. Panels: summary line under the heading; no repeated values.
7. Empty values: `—` for missing hours and bounds; `-` for the no-foreman row as the ticket names it.
8. Same labels as the IWP log: `IWPs`, `State`, `Hours`, `Subject`.
9. 80 and 170 columns: `mount 80` and `mount 170` with no line over the width; nothing truncated.
