## Deviations
none. Two points of reading, not deviations from scope:
- STEPS 2 opens `with _service_errors():` before the `try`, and STEPS 4 opens the unit of work
  separately. I put the unit of work inside the same `with _service_errors():` block, because
  `apply_plan` raises `ImportRefusedError`, a `ServiceError`, and the Acceptance test expects its
  `7 row(s) cannot be imported` text on stderr with exit 1. Without the wrap that error would print
  a traceback.
- The `return` on the dry-run path sits inside the `with _service_errors():` block. It exits both
  context managers normally, so nothing is printed twice and nothing is written.

## Learnings (proposed)
- The ticket's STEPS nest `_service_errors()` around the mapping read but leave the end of the block
  unstated. Covering the unit of work too is what makes a `ServiceError` from `apply_plan` print as
  `error:`. A future STEPS line for the import commands could say so explicitly.
- `plan_import` and `apply_plan` live under `packages/tl-modules/documents/src/tl_mod_documents/imports/`,
  not under a `tl-mod-documents` path. The worktree branch already had T12b–T12d merged, so nothing was missing.
