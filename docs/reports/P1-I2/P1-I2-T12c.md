## Open questions / Blocked
- Edge case not covered by the STEPS (not changed): a hand-built `ImportRow` with a blank `number` and no `problems` of its own reaches step 3 with `number == ""`, so `registry.find_document(conn, scope, "")` is called and the row lands in `plan.new`. `read_rows` cannot produce such a row (a blank number always carries "the document number is blank"), and no provided test builds one, so the behaviour is unexercised. If the supervisor wants it refused, a `if not row.number` clause in step 2 would be the place; I did not add it because the ticket says to follow the STEPS and not to widen the change.
