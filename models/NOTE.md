# models/ provenance

## AC20-FZK-Haus.ifc (added as the "richer" model, IFC4, 2.5 MB)

- Source: https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/blob/v0.9.0/docs/ifcopenshell-cpp/models/AC20-FZK-Haus.ifc
  (fetched with `git clone --depth 1 --filter=blob:none` plus a sparse checkout of `docs/ifcopenshell-cpp/models`).
- Content: the KIT "FZK-Haus" sample house exported from ARCHICAD 20 (Karlsruhe Institute of Technology,
  Institute for Automation and Applied Informatics). Walls, slabs, beams, stair, 5 doors and 11 windows with
  17 IfcRelVoidsElement / 16 IfcRelFillsElement relations. It has NO MEP (no ducts or pipes).
- Licence: the file carries no licence header. It is distributed inside the IfcOpenShell repository,
  whose licence is LGPL-3.0-or-later (COPYING.LESSER in the repo root). The FZK-Haus model itself is
  published by KIT as a freely usable sample; check upstream before redistributing outside this reference project.

## Process-Plant.ifc (synthetic, generated; `?model=process-plant`)

- Source: none. Generated by `tools/make_process_plant_ifc.py -o models/Process-Plant.ifc` (default `--seed 1`), a deterministic
  script (same seed, same bytes). Converted with `tools/ifc2glb.py` to `web/public/models/process-plant.{glb,json}`.
- Content: a small pump and heat-exchanger process unit. IFC4, metres, Z up. Building "Process Unit 100" with four storeys
  (Grade +0.0, Platform +3.5, Rack tier 1 +5.0, Rack tier 2 +8.0). A two-tier steel pipe rack about 60 m long (6 m bays), a
  stair and railed platform, a control/MCC building with walls, doors, windows and spaces, four pumps with motors, two shell
  and tube exchangers, two vertical drums, 452 pipe segments and 437 fittings in 11 distribution systems, 31 valves, 19 sensors,
  cable tray, luminaires and two switchboards.
- Counts (1,522 products, 1,514 in the sidecar: the converter skips 6 openings and 2 spaces): IfcPipeSegment 452,
  IfcPipeFitting 437, IfcSlab 136, IfcMember 100, IfcBuildingElementProxy 84, IfcBeam 77, IfcFooting 36, IfcValve 31,
  IfcCableCarrierSegment 30, IfcColumn 28, IfcPlate 22, IfcLightFixture 20, IfcSensor 19, IfcRailing 16, IfcOpeningElement 6,
  IfcWall 5, IfcElectricMotor 4, IfcPump 4, IfcDoor 3, IfcWindow 3, IfcHeatExchanger 2, IfcElectricDistributionBoard 2,
  IfcTank 2, IfcSpace 2, IfcStair 1.
- Licence: generated, no external source, so no licence beyond the repository's.
- Not a design: the line routes, sizes and tags are plausible placeholders. Some pipes touch equipment and the bracing crosses
  the piping. IFC4 has no PAVEMENT class (paving is IfcSlab BASESLAB); IFC4 pipe fittings have no TEE or REDUCER (JUNCTION and
  TRANSITION are used); IFC4 valves have no GATE or GLOBE (ISOLATING and REGULATING are used).
