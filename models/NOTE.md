# models/ provenance

## AC20-FZK-Haus.ifc (added as the "richer" model, IFC4, 2.5 MB)

- Source: https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/blob/v0.9.0/docs/ifcopenshell-cpp/models/AC20-FZK-Haus.ifc
  (fetched with `git clone --depth 1 --filter=blob:none` plus a sparse checkout of `docs/ifcopenshell-cpp/models`).
- Content: the KIT "FZK-Haus" sample house exported from ARCHICAD 20 (Karlsruhe Institute of Technology,
  Institute for Automation and Applied Informatics). Walls, slabs, beams, stair, 5 doors and 11 windows with
  17 IfcRelVoidsElement / 16 IfcRelFillsElement relations. It has NO MEP (no ducts or pipes).
- Licence: the file carries no licence header. It is distributed inside the IfcOpenShell repository,
  whose licence is LGPL-3.0-or-later (COPYING.LESSER in the repo root). The FZK-Haus model itself is
  published by KIT as a freely usable sample; check upstream before redistributing outside this reference project.

## Process-Plant.ifc (synthetic, generated; `?model=process-plant`)

- Source: none. Generated by `tools/make_process_plant_ifc.py -o models/Process-Plant.ifc` (default `--seed 1`), a deterministic
  script (same seed, same bytes). Converted with `tools/ifc2glb.py models/Process-Plant.ifc -o web/public/models/process-plant`
  to `web/public/models/process-plant.{glb,json}`. The same run writes the ground-truth file `models/Process-Plant.truth.json`.
- Content: a feed / pump / heat-exchanger process unit. IFC4, metres, Z up. Building "Process Unit 100" with four storeys
  (Grade +0.0, Platform +3.5, Rack tier 1 +5.0, Rack tier 2 +8.0). All equipment stands south of a two-tier steel pipe rack
  (60 m, 6 m bays, plus a 5 m battery-limit bay at the west end). Flow: crude feed (battery limit) to drum V-301, pumps P-101A/B,
  steam heater E-201 (process in the channel, steam and condensate on the shell), separator V-302 (vapour to the flare header),
  pumps P-102A/B, cooler E-202 (process on the shell, cooling water in the channel), product to the battery limit.
  Sizes follow typical proportions: pumps 2.0 m long on a 2.3 x 1.0 m baseplate with the shaft 0.7 m above the foundation,
  exchangers 1.0 m shell diameter, 5.5 m long, on two saddles, drums 2.4 m diameter, 5.0 m tangent to tangent on a 1.6 m skirt
  (7.0 m overall), control/MCC building 24 x 11 x 5.5 m (control room and MCC room, MCC double door 2.0 x 2.4 m).
  Also an operating platform with stair and railings north of the rack, 24 luminaires on the rack columns, a cable tray from the
  MCC panel through the east wall, up to rack tier 2 (continuous to the east end) and with a south trunk and drops to the four motors.
- Battery-limit convention: the BL is the west end of the rack, frame BL at x = -5 (marker board BL-W between its two columns).
  Seven lines cross it (crude feed, steam, condensate, cooling water supply and return, product, flare): each ends at x = -5.4
  on an isolation valve (about 2.5 m inside the frame) and a spectacle blind (IfcPipeFitting USERDEFINED), and sits on the BL frame
  beams. Everything else terminates on an equipment nozzle (a short flanged IfcPipeFitting that touches the body) or a tee on
  another line (suction and discharge headers of the pump pairs). The flare header goes to the battery limit (off-site flare), so
  the unit has no stack and nothing stands free above grade. Rack branches leave 0.7 m beside a column line (the columns are in
  the way on the column line) and rise or drop in risers 0.5 m outboard of the south column row, each with guide arms on a post.
- Self-checks in the generator (it exits non-zero when one fails): every pipe-spool end lies within 0.02 m of another pipe element,
  fitting, valve, nozzle, tee or blind; every run end is a nozzle, a tee or a spectacle blind; every nozzle touches its equipment;
  no two bodies overlap by more than 30 mm unless it is an intended contact (sampled test on oriented boxes and cylinders); no run is
  unsupported over more than 6 m; the cable tray is one connected run reaching MCC-101, the wall opening and the motors; luminaires
  touch a column; equipment rests on its base. It prints a summary.
- Counts (1,305 products, 1,294 in the sidecar: the converter skips 9 openings and 2 spaces; the truth file has 1,294 entries):
  IfcPipeFitting 260, IfcPipeSegment 248, IfcSlab 152, IfcMember 150, IfcBuildingElementProxy 101, IfcCableCarrierSegment 85,
  IfcBeam 84, IfcFooting 38, IfcColumn 30, IfcValve 30, IfcSensor 24, IfcLightFixture 24, IfcPlate 24, IfcRailing 16,
  IfcOpeningElement 9, IfcWall 5, IfcWindow 5, IfcPump 4, IfcElectricMotor 4, IfcDoor 3, IfcSpace 2,
  IfcElectricDistributionBoard 2, IfcHeatExchanger 2, IfcTank 2, IfcStair 1; 11 distribution systems.
- Ground truth (`Process-Plant.truth.json`, format `xray-truth/1`): per GlobalId the name, IFC class and the intended discipline,
  sub-discipline and MasterFormat division from `web/public/classification/default-schema.json`, plus `alt` codes for the 225 genuinely
  ambiguous elements (mostly pipe and tray supports STR/PIP steel 05 vs 40, flare PRC vs UTL, nozzle flanges, pump motors, baseplates and
  saddles). The labels are the design intent set where each element is created in the generator; they are not computed from the
  classifier rules and are not in the IFC. Distribution: PIP 642, CIV 230, STR 208, ELE 160, INS 27, ARC 26, GEN 1. How the ambiguous
  cases were decided is in the file's `convention` text. The rules (`web/src/classify/rules.ts`) disagree with it on pumps, drums,
  exchangers (MEC/PLB equipment), steam, cooling water and condensate lines (MEC/HYD, PLB/SUP), supports (STR/BRC) and leave 13 equipment-part
  proxies unresolved; `web/tests/classify-civil.test.ts` records what the rules say today.
- Licence: generated, no external source, so no licence beyond the repository's.
- Not a design: sizes and layout are plausible, but there is no P&ID, hydraulics, stress, hazard or code work behind it; line numbers, tags,
  duties and the 7 rack lines are placeholders, and the east 10 m of the rack are spare. Supports are placed by a rule (spacing 4 m) and
  checked, not engineered. IFC4 has no PAVEMENT class (paving is IfcSlab BASESLAB); IFC4 pipe fittings have no TEE, REDUCER or BLIND
  (JUNCTION, TRANSITION and USERDEFINED are used); IFC4 valves have no GATE or GLOBE (ISOLATING and REGULATING are used).
